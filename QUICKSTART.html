CorridorKey 2.0 Quick Start

CorridorKey 2.0 puts Corridor Digital's AI keyer inside a Blender material, so a footage plane shows its key in the viewport and in renders. This page gets you from a plane to a rendered key.

Where things are
  • 3D Viewport: press N and open the CorridorKey 2.0 tab. Most of the work happens here.
  • Right-click a plane: Set Up CorridorKey 2.0.
  • Shader Editor: Add > Texture > CorridorKey 2.0, with its controls in the sidebar's Node tab.
  • The ? button in the panel opens this guide.

1Pick an engine

CorridorKey 2.0 can run on either of two engines. The Engine menu at the top of the panel picks one; Automatic uses Corridor's add-on when it's enabled and the free one otherwise.

EngineWhat you need
Corridor Add-on (paid)Corridor Digital's CorridorKey add-on, installed and enabled in Blender.
Free (GitHub)Windows with an NVIDIA GPU. Click Install CorridorKey 2.0 (free): it downloads the open-source CorridorKey 2.0 with PyTorch and its models (several GB) in a window you can watch.

2Save your file

Save the .blend in your project folder, near the footage, before you key anything. Keys are saved in a CorridorKey folder next to the .blend, and the footage is found by a path relative to it.

Don't work from an autosave in Temp or Recover.

Its relative paths point into Temp, so the footage and keys go missing and Blender shows pink frames. The panel warns you when this happens.

3Set up the plane in one click

Select the plane that shows your footage, then click Set Up CorridorKey 2.0. It:

  • puts a CorridorKey 2.0 node between the footage texture and the shader
  • uses the plane's matte as the Alpha Hint, if it has one (Jetset and Autoshot planes usually do)
  • turns off a compositor key that would render over CorridorKey's
  • previews the frame you're on, which loads the model in the background

The Getting Started checklist at the top of the panel ticks off the engine, footage, saved file and Render Folder, with a button beside anything still to do. It disappears once all four are done.

4Preview

The first preview takes about 25 seconds while the model loads. After that a frame takes about a second, as long as the model stays loaded.

  • Preview Frame keys the frame you're on.
  • Live keys each frame you stop on.
  • Quick Preview keys each frame on its own, which is faster but rougher. Renders always use the full settings.
  • Cache Ahead, with Live on, keys frames outward from the playhead while you're not playing, a batch at a time, up to the Radius.
  • To preview a stretch of frames, use Some Frames in the Render box and click Preview. Nothing is written to your key.

Previews are cached for the exact settings you made them with. Changing a setting starts a new cache, and the trash button clears the old ones.

Reading the timeline
Blue: the rendered key. It grows frame by frame while a render runs. Green: previewed with the current settings. Orange: being keyed right now.

5Alpha Hint (optional)

An Alpha Hint is a rough black-and-white matte of what to keep. It helps with hard shots such as hair, motion blur or a spill-heavy screen. Everything about it is in the Alpha Hint section at the bottom of the panel: the hint itself, the engine's hint settings, and two ways to make or improve one.

If your plane has no matte, the Alpha Hint Maker can make one with Meta's SAM 3:

  1. Click Install SAM 3. It's free under Meta's SAM License, a download of about 7 GB.
  2. Type what to keep, for example person.
  3. Choose All Frames or Single Frame.
  4. Click Make Alpha Hint. It plugs itself into the node when it's done.

Screen Key, for green that's left behind

If patches of screen stay in the key (shadowed corners, the floor, edges of the set), the Screen Key box adds a simple green or blue keyer:

  • Set the screen colour: click the colour field and use its eyedropper on the screen in your footage, or click Green or Blue for a typical screen.
  • Turn on Show Matte. White is kept and black is taken out. Lower Threshold until the leftover screen goes black but your subject stays white. Softness sets how gradual the edge is.
  • Clean the Key applies it live: the key's alpha times the screen key. It renders, and locks for a render farm, the same way.
  • Bake into Alpha Hint multiplies it into your current hint for every frame (or uses it as the hint when there is none) and plugs the result in. CorridorKey then treats the leftover screen as background itself. Baking again after tuning starts from your original hint, not the last bake.

Sections you don't need fold away: click a section's title. The panel runs Render, Footage, Preview, the engine's settings, then Alpha Hint at the bottom. Alpha Hint Maker and Screen Key start folded, and a folded section still shows what it's doing in its title (rendering, baking, Live on).

6Render the key

Everything that renders is in the panel's Render box.

  • Whole Shot then Render Shot keys every frame into a new Render folder. When it finishes, Live and Cache Ahead switch off, because every frame is keyed.
  • Some Frames: set From and To (the clock button copies the timeline's preview range, or the frame you're on). Then Render them with the full settings, or Preview them. Current Key replaces those frames in your key (to fix a few frames). New Test Folder keeps them apart (to try settings).
  • While anything renders, the button becomes Cancel Render.
  • F12: with Key the Frame Before F12 on, the frame you're on is keyed first, so a still render always has a fresh key.
  • Delete Render moves the shown key to the Recycle Bin, and Open Folder shows it in Explorer. Below the panel, Reset puts the settings back to their defaults and clears the previews; your footage, Render Folder and finished key stay.

7Send it to a render farm

A render farm (SheepIt, a studio farm, another computer) doesn't have this add-on. Once the key is right, lock it:

  1. Render the whole shot, or at least every frame in the scene's frame range. Lock refuses while any of those frames is missing, and tells you which ones.
  2. Click Lock under Render Farm, at the bottom of the Render box. Lock All, or Render > Lock CorridorKey for Render Farm, locks every CorridorKey node in the file.
  3. Save, and send the .blend together with its CorridorKey folder, which holds the keyed frames.

A locked node is plain Blender nodes playing the finished key. It renders the same with or without the add-on, and the add-on leaves it alone. Unlock hands it back, with your settings, for previews and re-renders.

8If something looks wrong

You seeTry
Pink framesThe footage or key isn't where the .blend expects it. Use Find Missing Files and save the .blend in its project folder. A partial render shows the footage on the frames it didn't reach.
The render shows the raw plate or a different keyThe compositor is keying the plate again over CorridorKey's key. Click Turn Off Compositing in the warning.
A full path warningMake Paths Relative, so the project still works after you move or copy its folder.
Out of GPU memoryTurn on Low GPU Memory, lower the Internal Resolution, key a shorter range, or close other apps that use the GPU.
The first preview is slowThat's the model loading, about 25 s. Later previews are quick. After a while idle the model leaves the GPU and comes back in a few seconds.
